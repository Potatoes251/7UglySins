# 7 Ugly Sins

> *The Devil has a job for you. Don't disappoint him… or do.*

A narrative judgment game made in **Unity** during our **Game Week** in our 2nd year back at school, 
by a team of 7 (2 programmers, 2 game designers and 3 artists).

---

## About

You've just been hired by your new boss: **Satan**. Your job is simple. Souls arrive at your desk one after the other, and it's up to you to decide where they end up: **Heaven** or **Hell**.

Each soul comes with a **folder** containing a short description of the person and **5 feats** about their life. Read them carefully, weigh each deed, and pass your verdict.

But remember: your boss has his own opinion on who deserves what. Will you follow orders, or make your own judgment?

## How to Play

1. A soul arrives and says a few words.
2. Open their **folder** to read their description and their **5 feats**.
3. Each feat is **color-coded** and can be judged **individually**.
4. Move the **weights** onto the **balance** (Heaven on one side, Hell on the other) for each feat.
5. Once you're done, the soul goes where the balance tips:
   - More weight on **Heaven** → the soul ascends 
   - More weight on **Hell** → the soul descends 
6. The soul says a **different farewell** depending on their destination.
7. Repeat until the day is over.

### The Ending

At the end of your shift, you have a final conversation with your boss. The dialogue changes depending on whether you **obeyed Satan's expectations** or **followed your own judgment**.

## Features

- Narrative-driven gameplay with a dark-humor tone
- Intuitive balance and weight mechanic
- Color-coded feats, judged one by one
- Unique arrival and departure lines for every character
- Branching final dialogue based on your obedience
- Hand-crafted art and characters

## Getting Started

### Run the project from source

1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Potatoes251/7UglySins.git
   ```
2. Open **Unity Hub** and click **Add project from disk**.
3. Select the cloned folder (make sure you have Unity 6.2.2 installed).
4. Open the main scene located in `Assets/Scenes/FinalScene` and press **Play**.
